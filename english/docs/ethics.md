# Ethics and Responsible Use of Code

## Primary purpose of this project:

This project has an exclusively educational and research purpose. All experiments are conducted strictly to study mathematical properties, signal-analysis methods, and cryptographic models in controlled environments.

## Data

This project does not use:

- private keys belonging to other people;
- signatures or messages originating from third parties;
- confidential data;
- data obtained without permission;
- computer systems belonging to other people or organizations.

The mathematical component involving various programs is intended to graphically visualize results and does not require external information.

ECDSA signatures are used experimentally on keys generated by the team.

No real systems are attacked, and no real private keys belonging to anyone outside the team are used.

The leakage system is also generated and documented by the team, and noise is introduced under controlled conditions.

The CNN neural network is trained only by the team on data generated or measured during controlled experiments.

Key recovery, HNP methods, and lattice reduction are used only on what the team can generate, document, and control.

## Current limitations:

The project does not seek to:
- compromise the computer systems of third parties;
- recover real private keys;
- collect data without authorization;
- exploit vulnerabilities in real systems;
- obtain or use confidential information.

## Fundamental principle:

The fundamental principle of the project is:

> Research must be conducted in a controlled environment, using data and systems over which the team has usage rights, and results must be presented for educational and scientific purposes.
