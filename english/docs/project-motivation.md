# Project Motivation

The need for knowledge has been and will continue to be the basis of
human cognitive development. Computer science is one of the modern
methods through which a civilization can build and sustain its
development.

Our project represents the beginning of a journey with a destination
that remains unknown. Initially, we believed that this test of mental
endurance would help us discover a new passion that could help us in
the future. Surprisingly, our intentions proved true.

The more you work, the more acceptable things that create discomfort
become. Patience and the desire to progress are essential virtues of
an individual. The first step toward obtaining these characteristics
is to begin. Without a beginning and determination, there is no
result.

These words represent our motivation for building this project from
scratch. If we could go back in time and relive all the mental
blockages and lost hours of sleep, we would change nothing.
